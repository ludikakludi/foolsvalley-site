open position

land steward

live-in · starts september · ideally 3+ months

overview

This role involves caring for the land, gardens, and grounds at Fools' Valley. This is a live-in position — accommodation on-site is provided, along with meals, and you'll be able to attend activities, events, and offerings held during residencies. Ideally suited for someone who can commit to at least 3 months.

responsibilities

  • Maintain gardens, growing areas, and outdoor communal spaces
  • Plan and manage planting, harvesting, and seasonal land care
  • Care for the chickens, including feeding, coop maintenance, and egg collection
  • Coordinate composting systems and integrate them with garden/soil needs
  • Manage irrigation, watering schedules, and basic land infrastructure
  • Care for the pond, including maintaining its cleanliness and health
  • Involve and coordinate residents who want to help with gardening or land work
  • Maintain paths, outdoor furniture, and general grounds upkeep
  • Monitor and manage local ecosystems, wildlife considerations, or permaculture practices
  • Coordinate tools and equipment used for land work, ensuring they're maintained and available
  • Report and address any land-related issues (erosion, drainage, pests, etc.)

requirements

  • Comfort with building and repairing things
  • Interest in working with the garden and planting
This job can be split into a gardener + groundskeeper — so if you only have repair/construction skills and interests, or only gardening, don't hesitate to contact us.

big bonuses

  • Driving license
  • Portuguese language

conditions

  • Food and accommodation included, plus a salary
  • Access to activities, events and offerings held during residencies

start date

September
