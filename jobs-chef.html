open position

chef

live-in or commute · starts last week of september · 5 days/week

overview

This role involves preparing meals and sourcing ingredients for our community, working 5 days a week. You'll cook lunch and dinner for residents — breakfast is self-serve from a stocked kitchen — and there's a lot of space for creativity in what kind of dishes you cook.

responsibilities

  • Plan and prepare daily meals for residents, accommodating basic dietary restrictions
  • Manage the kitchen budget and grocery shopping/sourcing of ingredients
  • Receive food and grocery deliveries and sort/organise them appropriately
  • Manage food inventory and minimise waste
  • Organise meal prep by volunteers
  • For residencies organised by external teams, communicate with their organisers to accommodate specific budgets and preferences
  • Accommodate special occasions, events, or gatherings with appropriate meal planning
  • Ensure kitchen equipment is well-maintained and report any issues

location

  • Option 1: live on site at Fools' Valley (Sobral de Monte Agraço, Portugal · Lisbon area)
  • Option 2: commute 5 days/week from nearby areas

conditions

  • Salary, plus food included
  • Accommodation included if you live on site

start date

Last week of September

interested? write to slowreply@foolsvalley.com with a few words about yourself and how you like to cook.
