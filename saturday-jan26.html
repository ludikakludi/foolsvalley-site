001 / schedule
11:00 – 14:00

somatic communication

from thinking mind to living body. the first part of the class will be about relocating the sense of self from head to hara (japanese concept). in the second part we'll explore how to listen and move together, learning to follow and direct the emergence of dance.

with francisco borges
14:00 – 16:00

contact improvisation jam

open space to explore physical listening and shared movement intelligence through contact improvisation.

with francisco borges
16:30 – 18:30

transformational connection / circling

a live, present-time practice where a small group trains attention on what's happening right now—sensations, emotions, thoughts, impulses, and the subtle signals between people. like contact improvisation, but instead of exchanging weight and momentum, we exchange attention, honesty, and impact.

with einar boson & liza simonova
19:00
