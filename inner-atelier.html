the inner atelier

embodied creativity, presence & play at fools' valley

a retreat for those who long to slow down, reconnect with their creative aliveness, and remember the intelligence of the body.

001 / the invitation

Set in the wild beauty of fools' valley, The Inner Atelier is an intimate retreat exploring creativity not as performance, but as a natural way of being. Through movement, observation, conversation, art and time in nature, we explore creativity as something available to everyone — a natural expression of being alive.

Through movement, yoga, meditation, relational practices, drawing, photography, voice, psychodrama, and play, we will open space for deeper presence, expression, connection, and joy.

This is not about becoming "better." It is about becoming more available: to your body, your intuition, your imagination, other people, and the subtle beauty of the moment.
002 / the place

fools' valley offers a rare kind of quiet: wild, simple, and alive. Surrounded by nature, the retreat invites a slower rhythm — mornings in the body, afternoons of creative exploration, evenings of conversation, silence, laughter, and integration.

The valley itself becomes part of the practice: a place to walk, breathe, listen, notice, and remember that creativity often returns when we stop chasing it.

003 / why this retreat

In a world of constant stimulation, performance, and speed, many of us have forgotten how to listen — to the body, to intuition, to silence, to one another, and to the subtle creative impulses that want to move through us.

The Inner Atelier is an invitation to step outside the noise and return to a more spacious way of being. Not by forcing insight, but by slowing down enough for something natural to reawaken: presence, play, sensitivity, imagination, and the courage to be more fully alive.

Each day will invite a balance of structure and spaciousness: immersive morning practices, creative and relational explorations, time in nature, shared reflection, and moments to rest, wander, integrate, and simply be.

Together, we will create a space that is gentle, honest, alive, and spacious — a place to listen more deeply, play more freely, and return to the creativity already living within you.

004 / the retreat experience

move

Begin each day with yoga, Embodied Flow, breath, meditation, and somatic practices that bring you back into the body.

create

Explore drawing, slow photography, movement, voice, and improvisation as simple doorways into your natural creativity.

relate

Practise deeper listening, honest expression, boundaries, consent, witnessing, and meaningful connection.

play

Enter the world of psychodrama, embodied imagination, relational games, and spontaneous group exploration — with lightness, depth, and humour.

There will also be spacious time to rest, wander, integrate, and simply be.

005 / is this for you

this retreat may be for you if…

  • You feel creatively blocked, overstimulated, or disconnected from your body.
  • You long for more space, beauty, depth, and aliveness in your life.
  • You are tired of self-improvement spaces that feel too goal-oriented, intense, or performative.
  • You want to reconnect with creativity in a way that feels embodied, honest, playful, and human.
  • You are drawn to intimate group spaces where depth, humour, tenderness, and spontaneity can coexist.
  • You want to be guided by experienced facilitators while still having time to rest, wander, and listen inwardly.

what you may leave with

  • A deeper sense of ease and presence in your body.
  • A renewed relationship with your creativity — not as pressure, but as a source of vitality.
  • More trust in your intuition, impulses, and inner timing.
  • A felt experience of meaningful connection, honest expression, and relational play.
  • Simple embodied practices you can return to in everyday life.
  • The memory of having given yourself time, space, beauty, and permission to be more fully alive.

007 / for whom

This retreat is for artists and non-artists, seekers and skeptics, sensitive souls, facilitators, creatives, therapists, and anyone who feels called to reconnect with their body, imagination, intuition, and capacity for meaningful connection.

No previous experience is needed — only curiosity, openness, and a willingness to meet yourself and others with honesty and play.

008 / facilitators

Diana

Diana is a filmmaker, photographer, and embodiment facilitator interested in the art of presence. Her work explores how movement, creativity, and attention can become pathways back to ourselves and to one another.

Drawing from yoga, Embodied Flow, meditation, photography, drawing, and improvisational practices, she creates experiences that invite curiosity into the creative impulses that emerge when we become less concerned with performance and more available to experience and connect.

Her facilitation combines meditation, yogasana, embodiment, artistic exploration, and reflective inquiry to create spaces that are playfully human.

Dr Swan Dao

Dr Swan Dao is a psychotherapist, speaker, and experience designer dedicated to the transformative power of art, embodiment, and human connection.

With a PhD on self-transformation and system change from the University of Cambridge, training at the Jacques Lecoq School of Physical Theatre, and over 15 years of experience facilitating retreats, workshops, and immersive learning experiences worldwide, Swan integrates expressive arts, psychodrama, relational practices, somatic work, and depth psychology.

He is the Founder and Director of the Institute of Devotional Arts, where he designs and leads transformative experiences for healing, creativity, and human flourishing.
