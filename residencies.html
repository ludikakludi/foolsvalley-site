residencies

2026

for those ready to become a fool — to sacrifice the intellect to the Unknown. not just the passive sacrifice of an empty mind, but the active sacrifice of setting out on the fool's journey of openness and curiosity.

a sanctuary for creative weirdos. a place where nothing and everything can be sacred. every idea within reason (yes) is up for serious discussion or a good joke.

writers who need to finish the thing. renegade scientists. artists developing something they can't yet name. researchers of consciousness — human and machine. builders of futures they'd actually want to live in.

002 / how it works

schedule is co-created. we encourage everyone to host practices, workshops, salons — on what you know and what you don't know about.

three meals a day by our chef, with 1-3 cooking and cleaning shifts by the residents. arrival day is monday. minimum stay: 2 weeks.

€20/day for food and bills. private rooms from €150-525/week (less if monthly), dorms from €70/week. room options →

003 / transformational connection

for those interested in getting meditation, embodiment, and relational practice out of retreat spaces and into a co-created co-living environment.

five professionally facilitated circling transformational connection sessions a week at transformational connection hub and at the dancehall.

everyone in the valley is welcome to join. for those coming primarily for transformational connection who expect to join most sessions and are interested to practice throughout the day, you can choose to stay in the transformational connection hub.
