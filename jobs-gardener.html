open position

gardener / land regeneration

live-in or commute · flexible start

overview

Re-establish the garden and lead a tree planting and land regeneration effort at Fools' Valley, a 16-hectare retreat and research center north of Lisbon. You'll assess the land, develop a plan for the growing areas and for reforestation, and then implement it in phases, drawing on residents and volunteers for support.

good fit if you

  • Have practical experience in gardening, permaculture, agroforestry, or regenerative land management
  • Are comfortable designing a plan and adapting it as you learn the site
  • Enjoy working outdoors and guiding others in practical land work
  • Desirable: familiarity with the Mediterranean climate, local soils, and native or well-adapted species

responsibilities

  • Assess the land: soil, water, existing vegetation, erosion
  • Develop a plan for re-establishing the garden and growing areas
  • Design a tree planting and land regeneration plan, focused on species selection and placement
  • Establish and manage the garden: planting, harvesting, seasonal land care, irrigation schedules
  • Carry out the tree planting and regeneration work in phases
  • Work with composting and integrate it with soil-building needs
  • Coordinate residents and volunteers who want to contribute to gardening and land work

location

  • Option 1: live on site at Fools' Valley (Sobral de Monte Agraço, Portugal · Lisbon area) — accommodation and meals provided
  • Option 2: commute 5 days/week from nearby areas

conditions

  • Salary, plus food included
  • Accommodation included if you live on site

start date

Flexible
