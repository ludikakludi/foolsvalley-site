1. Who we are

Holy Mountain, Lda., a company incorporated in Portugal with registered office at Rua do Chafariz, n.º 4, Casal da Fonte das Pombas, Sapataria, 2590-407 Sobral de Monte Agraço, and taxpayer number (NIPC) 516907280, is the controller of the personal data described in this notice. We operate the local accommodation establishments (estabelecimentos de alojamento local) at Rua do Chafariz, n.º 4 and Rua Principal, n.º 2A, Casal da Fonte das Pombas, and Avenida Principal, n.º 2, Silveira, all in Sapataria in Sobral de Monte Agraço, Portugal.

You can contact us about data protection at dataprivacy@holymountain.pt or by post at the address above.

We have not appointed a Data Protection Officer, as we are not required to do so under Article 37 of the GDPR.

2. What this notice covers

This notice explains what we do with personal data about guests and about the person who makes a booking. We use guest data for two things only: to make and perform your booking, and to comply with obligations imposed on us by Portuguese law. We do not use it for marketing, we do not profile you, and we do not sell or share it for anyone else's purposes.

3. Where we get your data

We collect data:

  • directly from you, when you make a booking, check in, or contact us; and
  • from the person who made the booking, where you are a guest but not the booker. If someone booked on your behalf, they gave us your details.

A note on the accommodation bulletin. Portuguese law requires us to report the stay of foreign guests only — including citizens of other EU Member States — to the authorities within three working days of arrival, and again on departure. Portuguese nationals are not reported. We are also required to see a valid identity or travel document at check-in. We record only the data the bulletin requires; we do not retain a copy or scan of your document unless you ask us to keep one, and we do not use these data for any other purpose.

Accessibility and dietary information. If you tell us about a disability, medical condition, allergy or dietary requirement so that we can accommodate it, that may be data concerning health. We use it only to meet the need you have told us about, and only with your explicit consent under Article 9(2)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw that consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect anything done beforehand.

5. Do you have to give us this data?

Yes, for most of it. We cannot perform a booking without the data in the first row of the table above, and we cannot lawfully accommodate a foreign national without the data required for the accommodation bulletin. If you do not provide it, we will not be able to accept or continue your booking.

6. Who we share it with

We disclose personal data only to:

  • the Portuguese authorities, through the SIBA system operated by the Internal Security System (Sistema de Segurança Interna), which makes accommodation bulletin data available to the authorities responsible for immigration, border and internal security functions;
  • the Portuguese Tax Authority (Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira), for invoice reporting;
  • our accountant;
  • our payment service provider, which processes card payments;
  • our property management and booking software provider(s), who act as processors on our instructions under a contract complying with Article 28 GDPR;
  • our insurers and legal advisers, and where necessary a court or arbitral tribunal, if a claim arises;
  • any public authority to which we are required by law to disclose.

We do not share your data with anyone else, and we do not transfer it to any third party for that party's own marketing or commercial purposes.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of our service providers may process data outside the EEA. Where they do, we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. You may request a copy of the relevant safeguards from us using the contact details in section 1.

Where a dispute, claim or investigation is on foot or reasonably anticipated, we retain the relevant data until it is concluded. We delete or irreversibly anonymise data at the end of the applicable period.

9. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data and obtain a copy;
  • have inaccurate data rectified and incomplete data completed;
  • have your data erased, where we no longer need it and no legal obligation requires us to keep it;
  • restrict our processing in certain circumstances;
  • object to processing carried out on the basis of our legitimate interests, on grounds relating to your particular situation;
  • receive data you provided to us in a portable format, and have it transmitted to another controller, where processing is based on contract or consent and carried out by automated means;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

These rights are limited where we are under a legal obligation. In particular, we cannot erase accommodation bulletin data or tax records before the periods in section 8 have expired.

To exercise any right, contact us at dataprivacy@holymountain.pt. We will respond within one month, extendable by two further months where the request is complex, in which case we will tell you.

You may lodge a complaint with the Portuguese supervisory authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD) — Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1.º, 1200-651 Lisboa; www.cnpd.pt — or with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.

10. Automated decision-making

We do not make any decision about you based solely on automated processing, and we do not profile you.

11. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including access controls, encryption of data in transit, restriction of access to those of our personnel who need it, and contractual confidentiality obligations. Access to accommodation bulletin data is restricted to the personnel responsible for making the report.

12. Changes

We may update this notice. The current version is always available at foolsvalley.com/privacy.html and at the property. Where a change is material, we will tell you.

If you booked for other people

If you made a booking that includes other guests, you gave us their personal data. Please give each of them a copy of this notice, or tell them where to find it, before their stay. We are required to make this information available to every guest whose data we hold.

If CCTV is operated at the property, a separate notice applies and must be displayed at the point of capture.
