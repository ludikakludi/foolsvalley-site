open position

space keeper

live-in or commute · starts september · minimum 3 months

overview

Help care for our community space and support residents in keeping shared areas clean, functional, and welcoming.

core responsibilities

  • Orient and onboard guests and residents into the daily flow of the residency, and the rules and processes of the space
  • Manage ongoing tasks related to space and grounds maintenance: watering plants, feeding chickens, egg collection, trash removal — and manage residents and volunteers for the fulfilment of those tasks
  • Support the community in maintaining cleanliness of shared indoor and outdoor spaces: coordinate shared chore rotations, community cleaning schedules and recycling protocols, gently follow up when things fall through the cracks, and step in to complete tasks that haven't been carried out by others
  • Check departing residents' rooms for cleanliness and completeness, and attend to any outstanding cleaning or upkeep tasks left behind
  • Support communal laundry needs (e.g., shared towels and linens in common areas), ensuring items are washed when they accumulate
  • Restock essential supplies (cleaning products, toiletries, kitchen basics, etc.)
  • Track maintenance and repair needs, and serve as the main point of contact for day-to-day upkeep questions or issues
  • Model and gently reinforce shared-space care norms, helping new members get oriented
  • Help maintain a warm, welcoming environment for members and visitors

good fit if you

  • Are reliable, organized, and comfortable taking initiative without being asked
  • Have attention to detail and love making spaces nice
  • Enjoy practical work
  • Are comfortable taking responsibility for a large space, managing priorities in moments of limited time and labour
  • Would love to live in a shared environment, going through seasons of quiet and "a lot going on"

location

  • Option 1: live on site at Fools' Valley (Sobral de Monte Agraço, Portugal · Lisbon area)
  • Option 2: commute 5 days/week from nearby areas

If you live on site — accommodation and meals are provided, and you'll be able to attend activities, events, and offerings held during residencies. Ideally suited for someone who can commit to at least 3 months.

conditions

  • Salary, plus food included
  • Accommodation included if you live on site

start date

September
