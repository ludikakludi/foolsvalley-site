001 / the lab

we are bringing together contemporary dance, theater, experience design, psychosomatic techniques, relational & healing arts practices to research and develop frameworks for innovative, immersive performance and transformative ritual.

we are currently in the initial conceptual research phase. we are standing at the beginning of this journey and want to meet potential long-term collaborators who share our interests and experience.

our long-term vision is to form a dedicated, cohesive core crew that will continue to research, rehearse and perform in regular blocks each year.

002 / the residency: focus & intent

this initial residency is a space for deepening practice, artistic research, and collective experimentation.

we are investigating what happens when we combine aesthetic composition practices with methods that draw out and embody hidden or challenging psychological content emerging within us — personally, interpersonally and systemically.

over the week, we will introduce distinct practices from different traditions to see how they intersect, collaborate or create productive friction. some of these practices include:

  • contact improvisation
  • psychodrama
  • circling
  • authentic relating
  • clowning
  • butoh
  • corporeal mime
  • real time composition
  • process-oriented psychology
  • extended vocal techniques
  • improvisation in experimental & physical theatre

we hope to end our week having developed a psychospiritual game piece or ritual performance which will be offered to the larger community.

003 / who we are looking for

we are seeking artists, researchers and practitioners who possess solid experience and value the approach to collaborative creation. you should have a background in at least two of the fields below.

  • 01psychosomatic group processes or somatic research
  • 02psychodrama / constellations / radical partswork
  • 03contact improvisation
  • 04real time composition / instant composition
  • 05theater / clowning / performance
  • 06ritual- / experience design, LARP
  • 07social design

because we are building a foundation for the future, we are especially looking for individuals who are open for a long-term artistic commitment and are interested to explore at the intersection of deep psychological material and performance.
