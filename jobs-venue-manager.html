open position

venue manager / chief fool

the most substantial role · live-in or commute · start as soon as possible

overview

Oversee day-to-day operations and shape the cultural life and the future of the project — a hands-on, high-trust role.

responsibilities

  • Oversee daily operations across the property
  • Work closely with the existing small team: support chef, housekeeper, land steward, volunteers
  • Curate and create regular programming: residencies, events, salons, research gatherings
  • Build a wider community of residents, hosts, and event organizers around the project
  • Communicate with and host residents throughout their stay
  • Hold weekly meetings during Fools' Valley's own residencies and support events and activities offered by residents
  • Coordinate logistics, scheduling, and communication across team and guests
  • Serve as primary point of contact for residents, visitors, and collaborators

good fit if you

  • Have experience creating and/or leading a similar-sized project (retreat center, residency program, cultural venue, intentional community, etc.)
  • Are excited about community building and about living/working in a rural, shared environment
  • Ideally speak Portuguese
  • Are self-directed and comfortable managing a small team
  • Have an eye for hospitality and good programming
  • Are comfortable with ambiguity and enjoy shaping a role, not just filling one

location

  • Option 1: live on site at Fools' Valley (Sobral de Monte Agraço, Portugal · Lisbon area)
  • Option 2: commute 5 days/week from nearby areas

conditions

  • Salary, plus food included
  • Accommodation included if you live on site

start date

As soon as possible
