001 / the retreat

vipassana is the practice of seeing things as they are. you sit, you breathe, you watch what comes up and let it pass. we sit in silence several times a day.

authentic movement is its counterpart. eyes closed, a witness present, you let the body move from an impulse instead of an idea. no choreography, no right way. what was still on the cushion starts to move.

005 / small print

1. reservations

we'll get back to you with payment details once your booking is approved. space is reserved when paid in full.

2. refunds

6+ weeks out: 100% refund on accommodation. 2–6 weeks: 50%. under 2 weeks: no refund. daily fee is always fully refundable.

3. long-stay rates

weekly, two-week, and monthly rates kick in when you book for at least that period. extra days beyond the minimum are charged at the same per-day rate — e.g. if you book 10 days, you can get the weekly rate for all 10.

4. can I extend the booking later?

yes, you can extend the booking at the same rate as you booked it. e.g. if you booked two weeks first and then extend by another two weeks, you'll pay the two-week rate for both stretches. to get a monthly rate, you need to book for a month in advance.

5. what's per person vs. per room?

room rate = per room. daily fee, camping, and van spots = per person (since it covers mainly the use of communal facilities, not the accommodation itself).

6. can I opt out of food?

not fully. the daily fee also covers water, electricity, and kitchen fixed running costs. if our kitchen can't meet your dietary needs, we can offer you cooking and fridge space — and reduce a small part of the fee if needed.

7. kitchen & space care

we have a chef, but we ask everyone to help with 1–2 cooking/space care shifts per week. on sunday we have a collective cleaning session.

8. can I volunteer extra? how about for a discount / free stay?

we really welcome extra support. sometimes we also have discounted helper spots for people with building/carpentry/electrics/gardening skills, ask us about it.
