open position

grounds keeper & maintenance chief

live-in · starts september · minimum 3 months

overview

This role involves caring for the land, grounds, and buildings at Fools' Valley, including small repairs and general infrastructure upkeep.

It can be split into two positions — a Maintenance Chief  and a Gardener  — depending on skills and interests. If you're only drawn to repair/construction work, or only to gardening, don't hesitate to reach out.

This is a live-in position — accommodation on-site is provided, along with meals, and you'll be able to attend activities, events, and offerings held during residencies. Ideally suited for someone who can commit to at least 3 months.

responsibilities — grounds keeping and maintenance

  • Maintain buildings and general infrastructure, and carry out small repairs
  • Maintain gardens, growing areas, and outdoor communal spaces
  • Plan and manage planting, harvesting, and seasonal land care
  • Care for the chickens, including feeding, coop maintenance, and egg collection
  • Coordinate composting systems and integrate them with garden/soil needs
  • Manage irrigation, watering schedules, and basic land infrastructure
  • Care for the pond, including maintaining its cleanliness and health
  • Involve and coordinate residents who want to help with gardening or land work
  • Maintain paths, outdoor furniture, and general grounds upkeep
  • Monitor and manage local ecosystems, wildlife considerations, or permaculture practices
  • Coordinate tools and equipment used for repairs and land work, ensuring they're maintained and available
  • Report and address any maintenance and land-related issues (erosion, drainage, pests, etc.)

very desirable skills beyond repairs and/or gardening

  • Driving
  • Portuguese

conditions

  • Food and accommodation included, plus a salary
  • Access to activities, events and offerings held during residencies

start date

September — minimum 3 months commitment
