Fools' Research Lab

1 – 5 April 2026
Fools' Valley · Near Lisbon, Portugal

The inaugural Fools' Research Lab will investigate how embodied practices — contact improvisation, circling, constellations — shape human mental representational dimensionality and geometry in the face of highly complex moral and political scenarios, in contrast with purely verbal interventions.

…and explore the implications for the (in)adequacy of verbal constitutional alignment of large language models.

What to Expect

Embodied practice · getting weird · going meta on the getting weird · asking difficult questions with our bodies, our mental substrates, and machine-augmented intelligence · not resolving the contradictions · co-created sessions · good food · octopus cuddles
